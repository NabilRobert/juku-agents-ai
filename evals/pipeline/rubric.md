# Rubric: hand-grading weekly reports (P4)

Each week Arthur grades 5 reports. The target is at least 4 of 5. Code already
blocks invented numbers and bad citations (`pipeline/checks.py`). This rubric
covers what it can't judge.

A report passes only if it meets all three points. Grade the report as the
learner received it.

| Point | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Useful** | The learner knows what to do next week, and the advice fits their own week. | Generic advice that would fit anyone, or nothing to act on. |
| **Correct** | Everything matches the run's facts, and next week's words suit the learner's level. | A true number with a wrong meaning, or a plan that doesn't follow from the week. |
| **Tone** | Warm, honest and short. A hard week is named plainly and without blame. | Scolding, empty praise, a hard week hidden, or stiff wording. |

**How to grade:**
- A facts-only report (`pipeline/fallback.py`) isn't sampled: no agent wrote it.
- For each fail, write one line saying why. Anything seen 3 or more times
  becomes a task or a fixture (`docs/planning/standard-checks.md`).
